About Gift of the Givers

Gift of the Givers is South Africa's largest disaster response NPO. Founded in 1992 by Dr Imtiaz Sooliman in Pietermaritzburg, KZN after a spiritual calling to serve humanity (Gift of the Givers, 2025).

Our Mission

To provide rapid disaster response, medical aid, food, water and development support without government funding - relying entirely on public donations.

Our Timeline

  • 1992: Founded in Pietermaritzburg
  • 2005: First international deployment - Pakistan Earthquake
  • 2022: KZN Floods - 20,000+ families assisted
  • 2025: 45+ countries, largest NPO in Africa

Why Trust Us?

Audited annually, 100% donation-to-aid policy, transparency reports published online (SAICA, 2024).
